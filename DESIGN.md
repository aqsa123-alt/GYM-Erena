## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-premium, high-performance athletic identity tailored for high-output fitness, arena combat sports, and indoor tournament play. The aesthetic merges cinematic darkness with razor-sharp energy, evoking peak physical focus, power, and prestige. 

The visual style blends **High-Contrast Bold** with **Precision Dark Glassmorphism**:
- Deep obsidian and charcoal structural foundations that emulate arena tunnels, matte carbon gym fixtures, and low-light training spaces.
- High-intensity electric orange and volt-lime energy vectors that direct visual momentum toward booking actions, performance telemetry, and court reservations.
- Angular discipline: structural grids, angled badge motifs, and crisp hairline edges that reject overly playful or rounded forms in favor of athletic precision.
- Immersive atmosphere: subtle directional radial glows and smoky surface translucency that provide an arena-grade backdrop.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an immersive dark baseline punctuated by high-lumen athletic spectrums.

### Palette Architecture
- **Base Surfaces**: Canvas layers progress from `#0B0C0E` (pitch obsidian base) up through `#121316` (recessed canvas) and `#181A20` (surface container). Elevated panels sit on `#22252C`.
- **Primary Kinetic Accent (`#FF5500`)**: Electric athletic orange; reserved for high-impact actions, active session trackers, court availability markers, and primary buttons. Use hover shifts toward `#FF6A00` and focused edge glows at `#FF7700`.
- **Secondary Voltage Accent (`#CCFF00`)**: Acid volt lime; applied selectively for status indicators (e.g., "Live", "In Session", "PR Broken"), vital stat charts, and boxing round countdowns.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#FFB800`)**: Competition gold; reserved for membership tiers, championship standings, and tournament brackets.
- **Neutrals & High-Contrast Typography**:
  - `Text Primary`: `#FFFFFF` (100% white, commanding readability).
  - `Text Secondary`: `#9EA3AE` (neutral silver, muted athletic metadata).
  - `Text Tertiary`: `#5F6470` (structural labels and inactive controls).
  - `Hairline Borders`: Translucent charcoal (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) and energized orange (`rgba(255, 85, 0, 0.35)`).

## Typography

The typographic balance sets technical power against effortless legibility:
- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Technical, angular, and commanding. Headlines feature tight leading and slight negative tracking at large scales to evoke the precision of athletic stopwatches and high-end arena graphics.
- **Labels & Overlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`), creating distinct sport classification tags (e.g., `COURT 03 // BOOKED`, `WEIGHT DIVISION`, `ROUND 04`).
- **Body & Ergonomics (`Manrope`)**: Geometric yet humanist, providing pure legibility in dark environments for class schedules, sparring rules, trainer bios, and checkout summaries.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a structured fluid grid optimized for high-density sports schedules, court reservation matrices, and cinematic marketing showcases:
- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: Max container width of 1440px, centered with 3rem (`margin`) canvas gutters and 1.5rem (`gutter`) intra-column spacing.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Fluid canvas with 2rem margins and 1rem gutters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: 1.25rem edge margins (`margin-mobile`) with 1rem gutters (`gutter-mobile`).

Horizontal card carousels for courts or daily boxing schedules may intentionally bleed past right margins on mobile viewports to signal scroll momentum. Vertical spacing follows strict mathematical pacing driven by the `space-*` scale to maintain rapid scanning speed.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse, muddy dropshadows. Instead, depth is built through structured luminescence and glass tiers:
- **Tier 0 (Arena Floor)**: Base canvas `#0B0C0E`.
- **Tier 1 (Sub-Containers)**: `#121316` with 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Tier 2 (Glassmorphism Interactive Cards)**: Background `rgba(24, 26, 32, 0.75)` backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and framed by a hybrid hairline outline: top and left borders use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, while bottom and right use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`.
- **Tier 3 (Active / Elevated State)**: On hover or active selection, card borders switch to `rgba(255, 85, 0, 0.45)` coupled with an electric back-glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(255, 85, 0, 0.2)`.
- **Floating Modals & Drawers**: Solid dark carbon `#181A20` with a dual border ring: 1px inner `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` and an outer drop shadow of `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an aggressive, engineered aesthetic using **Soft (`1`)** roundedness. Corner radii remain intentionally restrained to project discipline, athletic tension, and architectural permanence:
- Base controls, buttons, text fields, and micro-badges use tight `0.25rem` (4px) radii.
- Content modules and booking cards scale to `0.5rem` (8px).
- Modals, hero media cards, and primary sheets terminate at `0.75rem` (12px).
- Circular pills (`border-radius: 9999px`) are strictly forbidden, with the exception of avatar portraits and floating pulse dots. Badges, tags, and buttons must preserve visible geometric corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Kinetic Energy)**: Background `#FF5500`, text `#0B0C0E` (bold `Space Grotesk`), sharp 4px radius. Hover triggers `#FF6A00` with an outer directional flare `0 0 16px rgba(255, 85, 0, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary (Arena Glass)**: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, border 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover elevates background to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` with border color shifting to `#FF5500`.
- **Ghost Action**: Text `#9EA3AE`, uppercase `label-md`. Hover shifts to `#FFFFFF` with an electric orange underline accent.

### Athletic Badges & Status Chips
- All badges are rendered in uppercase `label-sm` with 4px corner radii and `0.25rem 0.5rem` padding.
- **Sport Variant**: Charcoal fill (`#181A20`), 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, text `#FFFFFF`. Prefixed by a 6px neon dot (Orange for Boxing, Volt for Badminton, Silver for Gym).
- **Status "Live" / "Court Open"**: Semi-translucent lime tint (`rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.1)`), border `rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.3)`, text `#CCFF00`.

### Cards & Module Surfaces
- Built on Tier 2 Glassmorphism: `1.5rem` padding, matte dark charcoal background with frosted backdrop filter.
- Headers integrate high-contrast Space Grotesk section titling alongside compact metadata tags.
- Interactive court tiles present real-time grid slots that invert to solid `#FF5500` upon user reservation.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#121316`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Radius: 4px.
- Typographic input: 15px `Manrope`, color `#FFFFFF`. Placeholder: `#5F6470`.
- Focus State: Border transitions to `#FF5500`, complemented by an ambient outer glow `0 0 0 1px #FF5500`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- Custom square checkboxes with 2px radius. Unchecked: 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` on `#121316`. Checked: solid `#FF5500` with black geometric check mark.
- Segmented court/arena filters: Contained in an obsidian rail (`#0B0C0E`), with active segments highlighted via `#181A20` fill, white text, and a bottom 2px neon orange bar.

### Specialized Athletic Components
- **Court Schedule Grid**: Monospaced time rails paired with blocked time segments that display real-time statuses (Open, Reserved, Tournament Exclusive).
- **Fighter / Trainer Stat Cards**: Dark glass cards containing low-saturation duotone photography accented by bright orange telemetry markers, win/loss stats, and specializations.
